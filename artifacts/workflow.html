Quality gate

Before handoff, confirm the reviewed rows are complete and evidence-backed.

  • Reviewed rows show Agent Review Status = Reviewed
  • Approved rows show Agent Verified = True
  • High Priority rows have strong evidence
  • No original source columns were removed
Recommendation standard

The workflow prioritizes trust and quality over speed. AI surfaces recommendations and concerns; a human reviewer owns consequential decisions.

  • When uncertain, flag for Review
  • When evidence conflicts, flag Concern for human review rather than automatically rejecting
  • Recommend advance only when identity evidence is strong
