About

My foundation is experimental psychology: distinguishing observable behaviors from the underlying constructs they may reflect, then testing competing explanations.

My PhD on how people perceive, judge, and make decisions led to years of mixed-methods studies and UX product research.

I bring that rigor to AI-assisted research and product work, accelerating throughput while keeping verification, judgment, and accountability human‑owned.

I size studies to the decision and match claims to what the sample supports, stating the unit of analysis, the repeated-measures structure, and the uncertainty.
